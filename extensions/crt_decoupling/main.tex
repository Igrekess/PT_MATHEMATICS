\begin{abstract}
We prove two complementary theorems for the prime-sieve dynamical
system on $\mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}$.
\textbf{(Theorem~4.1, empirical form.)}~The mutual information
$I_{\pi_m^{\mathrm{emp}}}(\Phi_p\,;\,\Phi_q\,|\,G(\mu))$ between
observables on disjoint active CRT channels is constant under the
geometric flow $\mu \in [\mu_c, \infty)$ generating the Bianchi-I
metric $G(\mu)$ from the spectral data of the transfer matrix
$T_m$.
\textbf{(Theorem~4.2, idealised form.)}~Under the Ruelle/MME
measure $\pi_m^{\mathrm{Ruelle}}$, the same mutual information
vanishes exactly.
A closed-form expression for the empirical residual
$\mathcal{I}_{p,q}$ is derived (Corollary~4.3) and validated
against the corpus values $\mathrm{MI}(g\bmod 3, g\bmod 5) = 0.138$
bits and $\mathrm{MI}(g\bmod 3, g\bmod 7) = 0.283$ bits.

The geometric-invariance result has direct consequences for the
foundational debate on Bell-type non-locality: the observed
inter-channel correlations are arithmetic, not spatial, and the
emergent metric $G(\mu)$ does not couple disjoint modular fibres.
This complements the companion foundational paper
\cite{PT_HOLONOMY_NONLOCALITY}.

All algebraic results of this paper (Theorems~3.1, 3.2, 6.1, 6.2 and
Lemma~4.1) are formally verified in Lean~4 + Mathlib under the
namespace \texttt{PT.CrtDecoupling}; see
Section~\ref{ssec:formal_verification} for the correspondence table
and the audit (0~\texttt{sorry}, 0~\texttt{axiom}).
\end{abstract}
