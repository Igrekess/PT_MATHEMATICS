\subsection{Distributional capture of zeros}
\label{sec:verif_capture}

Test on $t \in [10, 70]$ with Abel--Hadamard regularisation $p^{-\eps}$.
For $\eps \in \{0.6,\,0.4,\,0.2\}$, the modulus $|F_\eps(t)|$
systematically grows by a factor of $2.7\times$ to $3.3\times$ at
the zeros of $\zeta$ when $\eps$ goes from $0.6$ to $0.2$ (signature
of a pole), against $1.4\times$ to $2.3\times$ at neutral points
(regularity). On $t \in [10, 70]$, $dt = 0.1$, we detect 41-52 local
peaks depending on $P_{\max} \in [3\,000,\, 100\,000]$; all 17
reference zeros are within $1.5$ units of a peak ($|\Delta| < 1.5$)
from $P_{\max} = 3\,000$.

\paragraph{$P_{\max}$ saturation.}
Capture saturates beyond $P_{\max} = 3\,000$: the median $|\Delta|$
decreases from $0.49$ ($P_{\max} = 3\,000$) to $0.36$ ($P_{\max} = 30\,000$)
then stagnates. The same statistic extended to $t \in [10, 200]$
gives $74/79$ zeros (94\,\%) at $|\Delta| < 1.5$, $38$ at
$|\Delta| < 0.5$, $7$ at $|\Delta| < 0.1$. The 5 missed zeros
belong to pairs $(\gamma_n, \gamma_{n+1})$ with gap below the
intrinsic width of an unregularised peak ($\sim 2\pi/\log\gamma$),
merged into a single peak. Extreme precision case:
$\gamma = 146.001$ captured at $|\Delta| = 0.001$. Systematic
sub-$0.1$ improvement requires applying explicit regularisation
$R_1$ or $R_2$, not increasing $P_{\max}$.

\subsection{Gaussian smearing regularisation and extended range}
\label{sec:verif_smearing}

With $\phi_\eps(t) = (\eps \sqrt{2\pi})^{-1} \exp(-t^{2} / 2\eps^{2})$ and
$\eps \in \{2,\,1,\,0.5\}$, the pole/neutral contrast is more
pronounced: $8\times$ to $10\times$ at the zeros against $2\times$
to $3\times$ at neutral points. Gaussian and Abel regularisations
are equivalent in the distributional sense (different regularising
kernels), but the former is numerically more sensitive.

\paragraph{Extended range $t \in [10, 500]$ (269 zeros).}
$R_1$ with $\eps = 0.2$ captures $267/269$ zeros at $|\Delta| < 1.5$,
$242/269$ at $|\Delta| < 0.5$ and $139/269$ at $|\Delta| < 0.1$.

The floor at $86.6\,\%$ is consistent with the structural limit of
$55$ merged pairs (gap $< 2\pi/\log\gamma$) out of $269$ zeros, i.e.\
$20.4\,\%$ of zeros belonging to a pair below the intrinsic model
resolution.

\paragraph{$\eps$-adaptive strategy tested.}
An $\eps$-adaptive strategy ($\eps \propto 1/\log\gamma$) was tested
in two forms: convolution on raw $|F|$ (peak collapse, falsified)
and convolution on complex $F$ (marginal compromise: median
$|\Delta|$ reduced to $0.080$ but coverage reduced to $76\,\%$ at
$|\Delta| < 1.5$). Fixed spectral-weight regularisation ($R_1$,
$\eps = 0.2$) remains the \textbf{global optimum}.
