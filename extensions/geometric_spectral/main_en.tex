\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The Theory of Persistence (PT), exposed in its companion article~\cite{PT_Article1_Foundations}, derives from a single arithmetic axiom~$\sper = 1/2$ a cascade structure on the active primes~$\{3, 5, 7\}$ and an arithmetic fixed point~$\mustar = 15$. This second article explores three complementary readings of that structure: (i) an \emph{algebro-geometric} reading that exhibits the canonical cascade as the unique algebraic curve in the Kontsevich--Norbury class satisfying three conditions of holonomy, activation, and self-consistency, and computes its genus $\genus = \mustar - 1 = 14$; (ii) a \emph{differential} reading that establishes that the Fisher--Bianchi metric induced by the cascade satisfies the Ricci soliton equation $\Ric + \Hess(f) = \lambda(\mu)\, \gPT$ with $\lambda(\mu) = -1/\mu^{4} + O(1/\mu^{5})$, coefficient~$-1$ exact; (iii) a \emph{spectral} reading that establishes the triple identity $1/8 = \sper^{2}/2 = \lambdaH = \DeltaMaslov$ relating the Higgs coupling and the Maslov phase of the Riemann zeros to the unique formal input of PT, together with an exact trace identity verified to $10^{-26}$. The more ambitious spectral-arithmetic program whose first stones these identities are (RH-PT conjecture) is sketched as perspective.
\end{abstract}
